# The teamrig name

teamrig's code is yours to use, change, share and sell under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The license gives no
right to the name (its section 6), so the name follows the rules below. They keep "teamrig" meaning the project's own
releases, so nobody takes a changed copy for the original. They cover "teamrig" and the names built on it, such as
"teamrig-pane".

## Fine without asking

- Say what your work is: "built on teamrig", "a plugin for teamrig", "works with teamrig", "a fork of teamrig".
- Hand out an unchanged copy of a teamrig release under its own name.
- Write about teamrig, review it or teach it, in posts, talks and videos.
- Keep the name inside your fork: its code, its history, your private builds.

## Needs a name of its own

- A changed copy you hand out or publish (a fork, a modified plugin, a service built on it) takes a name that is not
  "teamrig" or one easily mistaken for it ("teamrig Pro", "teamrigx"), and may say it is based on teamrig.
- Nothing may suggest the project made, checked or endorses work it did not.

## Keep with every copy

The [LICENSE](LICENSE) and the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file, unchanged (the license's section 4). Add your own notices beside
them, never in their place.

## Anything else

Open an issue in this repository before you use the name in a way this page does not cover.
